      Why these decisions

      Keep the gateway independent of the specialist. Make review a separate state from execution. Use reproducible synthetic fixtures. Start with a lightweight 2.5D concept to keep the mobile experience usable.

      Where the limits are

      No operating restaurant, paid employment claim, live customer data, production authentication, brewing validation or autonomous purchasing. Browser audit records are disposable illustrations.
