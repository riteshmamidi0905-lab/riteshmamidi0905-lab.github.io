NASHA AI LAB / ECOSYSTEM EXPLORER

A business concept.
An engineering laboratory.

NASHA AI Lab is my independent AI engineering ecosystem. The proposed NASHA brewery is a business concept that gives it a realistic scenario: how could AI help create, plan and operate a business, and how would we measure, secure and govern it?

Concept: the NASHA brewery is a proposal, not an operating business.In development: NASHA AI Lab.Not passed: the live model evaluation has not met its acceptance criteria (failed).Not deployed.
