    Experience E / Recruiter mode

    The engineering, in two minutes.

    Built by Ritesh Mamidi as an independent engineering project. NASHA AI explores how structured specialist services can support hospitality decisions while keeping protected actions behind a separate policy boundary.

    What runs

    Twelve deterministic callable specialists and three synthetic workflows in Python. THE WEIR is a separate gateway, not a thirteenth specialist. This page runs browser illustrations; it does not call a model or the backend.

    What was tested

    Engineering CI #132 at 2fc1000: 368 Python tests passed, one skipped; 15/15 deterministic synthetic workflow contract checks. These are not model-accuracy or real-world business metrics.

    Inspect engineering CI

    Why these decisions

    Keep the gateway independent of the specialist. Make review a separate state from execution. Use reproducible synthetic fixtures. Start with a lightweight 2.5D concept to keep the mobile experience usable.

    Where the limits are

    No operating restaurant, paid employment claim, live customer data, production authentication, brewing validation or autonomous purchasing. Browser audit records are disposable illustrations.

    Read implementation status correctly

    IMPLEMENTED: code exists. TESTED: the stated local checks passed. CI VERIFIED: the linked CI run passed at its stated commit. ILLUSTRATIVE: browser scenario or design concept. PLANNED: future work. NOT VERIFIED: evidence not established. Historical CI success does not verify new changes.
