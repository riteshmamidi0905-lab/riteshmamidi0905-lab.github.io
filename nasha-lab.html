04 / EVALUATION & LIMITATIONS

Measure the contract.
Keep the caveats.

Contract evaluation

Recorded handoffs check typed outputs, required fields and finite values. Synthetic negative cases include invalid inventory, missing quotes and nonfinite finance.

Governance evaluation

The preserved integration records argument binding, session binding, expiry rejection, replay blocking and audit tamper rejection. These are historical offline results, not production security certification.

Generalization limits

Author-created synthetic scenarios do not establish real demand, commercial outcomes, agent intelligence or third-party validation. Operational backend integration and human assistive-technology review remain open work.
