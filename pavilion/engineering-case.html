What Ritesh built

A configurable business model runs four synthetic operating weeks. Twelve named specialists own typed events, each with input and output record IDs. A browser can replay the schedule or read the same source book with scripts disabled.

From decision to evidence

  1. The opening budget, recipes, staff and seed parameterize the operations engine.
  2. An inventory shortage creates a purchase proposal. In the human-reviewed scenario, a visitor explicitly approves or rejects the outstanding request.
  3. Receiving posts physical units and paired inventory and cash entries. Costs, tax and refunds feed a daily trial balance and a seven-day financial close.
  4. The console recomputes task outcomes and handling costs from event minutes. VERITAS checks paid quantities and balanced postings against their source records.

Determinism

A configuration, seed and decision sequence reproduce the same records. A what-if run varies price, staffing or delivery time against a same-seed baseline. Both closes remain inspectable.

Presentation boundary

The floor reads a frozen snapshot. Handoffs match producer outputs to consumer inputs by record ID. Playback and camera changes cannot authorize a purchase or write a journal.

Verification and limits

Model checks reconstruct balances, match receipts to approvals, reproduce decisions and verify read-only playback. The evaluation score describes the simulation’s consistency checks. It does not benchmark an external AI model.

All activity is synthetic and rule-based. Handling time and cost use documented assumptions. This is an independent engineering demonstration without commercial operation or live AI execution.
