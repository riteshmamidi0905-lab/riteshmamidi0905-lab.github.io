# Living headquarters blueprint — proposed, not built by this review

The registry is a complete 2D engineering alternative. The following spatial design is conceptual and has no newly delivered architectural asset or owner visual acceptance.

Visitor route: founder/recruiter entry → central mission-control atrium → operational workrooms → proposed brewery/courtyard → engineering evidence. Direct recruiter, experience, project, contact and resume navigation remains available at every stage. No locked cinematic funnel or mandatory audio.

One coherent building should share structural bays, circulation, doors, glazing, scale and a warm limestone/metal/walnut palette. Workstations have functional surfaces, seating/standing clearance, actual displays and sight lines, rather than floating portraits. Initial camera views should establish people-scale, useful detail and legible operations before any twelve-room expansion.

| Station | Proposed work area and actual display purpose |
|---|---|
| AETHON | Mission-control strategy desk: demand requirements, cited options and proposal context |
| ZYBREW | Brewery planning center: batch schedule, fermenter availability and recorded capacity alerts |
| PYRIX | Culinary test bench near proposed kitchen: recipe/allergen consistency and draft test packs |
| VECTRA | Inventory intelligence room: stock evidence, discrepancy, shortfall and ledger view |
| NEXORA | Procurement desk adjacent to receiving: supplier alternatives and draft-order approval state |
| QUANTIX | Analytics/finance center: cost exposure and cited financial metrics |
| ECHON | Customer analytics station: feedback classes, stock-linked evidence and uncertainty |
| NOVARA | Communications review workspace: draft outreach/release status and policy constraints |
| SYNAPSE | Shared operations center: declared task dependencies, progress, blocked tasks and recovery |
| AEGIS | Governance/authorization center: exact request, policy outcome, human review and refusal |
| CORTEX | Knowledge evidence workspace: query, returned sources and retrieval outage |
| VERITAS | AI evaluation laboratory: check names, pass/fail evidence and release withholding |

Displays read the selected authoritative scenario snapshot. Historical recordings and the browser operating-day ledger must have separate, persistent labels. Proposed connectors indicate routes only; a working animation or tool action is never implied merely by a lit station.

Brewery entrance, landscaped founder courtyard, bar, visible brewing equipment, dining and open kitchen form a proposed hospitality destination, not an existing commercial venue. Preserve approved food/beverage concepts and Mango Habanero Gobi's appetizer classification. Inventory/receiving has a plausible physical connection to brewery storage. New environmental art requires its own fidelity review, provenance, measurements and approval; the simplified PR #13 scene is not promoted as premium production art.
